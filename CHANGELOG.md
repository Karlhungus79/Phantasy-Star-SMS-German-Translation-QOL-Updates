# Changelog

**English** | [Deutsch](#deutsch)

## v1.0 – 2026-10-30

First release.

- All dialogue, narration, system and battle messages, menus, title screen, name entry and credits headings in German; text fully proofread.
- Umlauts Ä/Ö/Ü added to the font; ROM expanded to 1 MB.
- Items, weapons, armour, enemies and spells keep their original English names.
- Quality-of-life features: OPTIONEN (EXP/Meseta 1x/2x/4x, faster walking incl. dungeons, random encounters on/off), battle macros, battle turbo (hold button 1).
